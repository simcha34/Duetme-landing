🎤 Duet Me

Where Voices Connect Before Faces Do

A voice‑first social dating experience that creates genuine connection through music – not swipes.

The Problem

Dating apps today are flooded with endless profiles and superficial swiping. Authentic emotional connections are rare and users become fatigued.

Our Solution

Duet Me shifts the focus to voice and emotion. Users connect through real-time musical duets, singing together before ever seeing each other.

Product Highlights

  • Choose a song or join a duet queue
  • Each sings a segment (verse/chorus)
  • Receive the final combined version
  • If chemistry sparks – continue in live video duet or chat

Target Market

Ages 16–35, music lovers, creatives and early adopters of voice‑based tech. Especially those tired of photo-first dating.

Market Opportunity

Global online dating market exceeds $10B

Over 1B karaoke/music app downloads

Rising demand for emotionally‑driven social experiences

Business Model

  • Freemium + premium features (voice filters, song catalog)
  • Paid duet invites with popular users
  • Sponsored song launches with artists/labels
  • Future: live events, influencer campaigns

Go‑To‑Market

  • Closed beta with university/music communities
  • Collaborations with TikTok/IG influencers
  • Music challenges and duet competitions
  • Paid social marketing campaigns
